Banana Bread Recipe

Jump to recipe

Description

Banana bread is a timeless, comforting treat that combines the natural sweetness of ripe bananas with a moist, tender crumb. With its golden-brown crust and soft, slightly dense interior, each slice offers a perfect balance of flavors—subtly sweet, with hints of vanilla, cinnamon, or even a touch of nutmeg.

Whether enhanced with a handful of crunchy walnuts, gooey chocolate chips, or left simple and classic, banana bread is versatile and satisfying. It’s the perfect way to turn overripe bananas into a delightful homemade baked good, ideal for breakfast, a mid-day snack, or dessert. Best served warm with a pat of butter, it fills your kitchen with a cozy, inviting aroma that feels like a hug in every bite.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • ½ cup butter
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 2 ⅓ cups mashed overripe bananas

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Lightly grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan.
  2. Combine flour, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl. Beat brown sugar and butter with an electric mixer in a separate large bowl until smooth. Stir in eggs and mashed bananas until well blended. Stir banana mixture into flour mixture until just combined. Pour batter into the prepared loaf pan.
  3. Bake in the preheated oven until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean, about 60 minutes. Let bread cool in pan for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack to cool completely.
