Pot Roast

Jump to Recipe

Description

Pot roast is the ultimate comfort food—a hearty, slow-cooked dish that’s bursting with flavor and warmth. It starts with a beautifully seared cut of beef, typically chuck or brisket, which becomes melt-in-your-mouth tender as it braises slowly in a rich broth infused with aromatic herbs, garlic, and onions.

Surrounding the beef are perfectly cooked vegetables like carrots, potatoes, and celery, which soak up all the savory juices, creating a medley of textures and flavors in every bite. The dish is often finished with a luscious, velvety gravy made from the braising liquid, bringing everything together with a touch of indulgence.

Pot roast is a nostalgic and soul-soothing meal, ideal for family dinners or cozy gatherings, offering the kind of warmth that turns any day into something special.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 (3 pound) boneless beef chuck roast, trimmed
  • 1 pinch seasoned salt, or to taste
  • 1 pinch onion powder, or to taste
  • ground black pepper to taste
  • 1 (14.5 ounce) can beef broth
  • 1 ½ tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 large onion, cut into 4 wedges
  • 4 large potatoes, peeled and cut into bite-size pieces
  • 4 carrots, peeled and cut into bite-size pieces

Instructions

  1. Gather all ingredients
  2. Heat oil in a pressure cooker over medium-high heat. Sear roast on all sides in hot oil until browned; season with salt, onion powder, and pepper.
  3. Pour in beef broth and Worcestershire sauce; add onion and seal the lid. Bring the cooker up to full pressure. Reduce heat to low, maintaining full pressure, and cook for 30 minutes.
  4. Use the quick-release method to lower the pressure. Stir in potatoes and carrots and seal the lid; bring the cooker up to full pressure and cook for an additional 15 minutes.
  5. Use the quick-release method again and transfer roast and vegetables to a serving dish.
